FinGenie Financial Guide

Personal Loans

A personal loan is generally an unsecured loan that can be used for purposes such as medical expenses, education, home improvement, travel, or debt consolidation.

Personal loan eligibility may depend on factors such as income, employment history, credit score, existing obligations, age, and the lender's internal policies.

Common documents requested for a personal loan application may include identity proof, address proof, income documents, bank statements, and employment-related documents.

Home Loans

A home loan is a secured loan used to purchase, construct, or renovate a residential property.

The property generally acts as security for the loan.

Home loan eligibility can depend on income, age, credit history, existing liabilities, employment stability, property value, and the lender's policies.

Credit Cards

A credit card provides a revolving line of credit.

The cardholder can make purchases up to the available credit limit and repay the outstanding amount according to the card issuer's terms.

Paying credit card bills on time can help maintain a healthy credit history.

CIBIL Score

A CIBIL score is a numerical representation of an individual's credit history.

Payment history, credit utilisation, length of credit history, credit mix, and recent credit enquiries can influence creditworthiness.

EMI

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment.

An EMI generally consists of both principal and interest components.

The EMI for a loan depends on the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure.
